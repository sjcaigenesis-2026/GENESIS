Before you build

Rules & regulations

The full rulebook is being finalized — here's the shape of it. Detailed PDF drops before registrations open.

Team size

3 to 5 members max per team.

On-ground presence

At least 2 members from each team must be physically present at their allocated place.

Built from scratch

All prototypes must be built during the event, from scratch — no pre-built submissions.

AI usage

Use of AI tools is allowed while building your prototype.

Eligibility

Open to school students up to Class 12 only. Cannot include organisers, volunteers, mentors, or jury.
