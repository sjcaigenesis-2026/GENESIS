Rules & Guidelines

Build with Purpose.
Compete with Integrity.
Create the Future.

Welcome to SJC AI Genesis 2026 — a platform where innovation meets responsibility. Every participant is expected to uphold the spirit of creativity, collaboration, and fair competition.

👥

Team Formation

  • Teams must consist of 3–5 members.
  • Every participant can be a member of only one team.
  • Team composition cannot be changed after registrations close unless approved by the Organizing Committee.
📍

Physical Attendance

  • At least 2 members of every team must remain physically present at the allocated venue throughout the event.
  • Teams must report before the reporting time. Late arrivals may miss important announcements or judging rounds.
💡

Build Original. Think Different.

Innovation is the heart of Genesis.

  • Projects should be primarily developed during the hackathon.
  • Existing frameworks, APIs, SDKs and open-source libraries are allowed.
  • Copying another team's project or presenting previously completed work is strictly prohibited.
🤖

AI Usage Policy

AI is your co-pilot — not your replacement.

Participants may freely use tools such as:

ChatGPT GitHub Copilot Claude Gemini Cursor AI Any other public AI assistant
  • Teams must understand and explain every part of their solution.
  • Judges may ask questions regarding any implementation.
  • Blindly AI-generated projects without understanding may receive lower scores.
⚖

Fair Play

The following will result in immediate disqualification:

Immediate Disqualification

  • Plagiarism
  • Stealing another team's idea or code
  • Tampering with another team's project
  • Offensive, harmful or illegal content
  • Unsportsmanlike behaviour
  • Misleading judges
🌐

Internet & Resources

Permitted

  • ✅ Open-source libraries
  • ✅ Public APIs
  • ✅ Official documentation
  • ✅ AI tools

Not Permitted

  • ❌ Private repositories belonging to other teams
  • ❌ Paid software obtained illegally
  • ❌ Unauthorized access to any system
💻

Development Rules

Participants are free to choose any technology stack including:

Web Development Mobile Apps Artificial Intelligence Machine Learning Robotics IoT Cybersecurity Cloud Computing Game Development Data Science

Innovation matters more than the programming language.

🚀

Submission Requirements

Every team must submit:

  • Working Prototype
  • Source Code
  • PPT / Demo
  • Problem Statement
  • Solution Description

Projects submitted after the deadline may not be considered.

🎤

Final Presentation

Each team will receive a limited presentation slot. Judges may evaluate:

✨ Innovation ⚙ Technical Excellence 🎯 Problem Solving 🎨 UI/UX 🤖 AI Integration 🌍 Real World Impact 🎙 Presentation Quality
🤝

Code of Conduct

Genesis is built on respect. Every participant is expected to:

  • Respect judges, volunteers and fellow participants.
  • Maintain professionalism.
  • Keep the venue clean.
  • Avoid abusive language or disruptive behaviour.

Failure to follow the Code of Conduct may lead to disqualification.

📷

Photography & Media

  • Photographs and videos may be captured throughout the event.
  • By participating, teams grant permission for media captured during the event to be used for promotional purposes by SJC AI Genesis and St. Joseph's College, Nainital.
🏆

Organizer Rights

The Organizing Committee reserves the right to:

  • Modify schedules if required.
  • Update rules whenever necessary.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Disqualify teams violating the guidelines.

The decision of the judges and organizing committee shall be final and binding.

Genesis Oath

Create Fearlessly. Build Responsibly. Learn Constantly. Respect Everyone.

The future isn't waiting. Build it.
